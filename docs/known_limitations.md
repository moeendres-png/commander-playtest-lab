# Known Limitations

- Real external XMage/Forge provider validation remains pending. No primary external provider has been selected; provider selection requires comparable evidence from the planned feasibility/spike phase.
- Current opponent decks are structural role profiles rather than card-by-card executable engine decks.
- Cosmic Spider-Man contains a synthetic mid-budget completion.
- Official precons are represented as fixed structural references; unknown local opponent upgrades remain uncertainty rather than invented exact data.
- Real-playtest calibration is inactive project scope. Optional compatible import/calibration capability may remain, but real games are not a release or roadmap requirement.
- Candidate inventory is a narrow local optimization snapshot, not the complete canonical collection.
- Tactical-oracle results are bounded fixtures and cannot establish full external rules correctness.
- Structural simulations abstract politics, threat perception and many exact card interactions.
