# Known Limitations

- External XMage validation remains pending.
- Current opponent decks are structural role profiles rather than card-by-card executable engine decks.
- Cosmic Spider-Man contains a synthetic mid-budget completion.
- Official precons are represented as fixed structural references; opponent upgrades are intentionally ignored.
- No manual-playtest calibration exists in the active product. Validation relies on structural simulations, Tactical Oracle checks, and explicitly executed external rules engines.
- Candidate inventory is a narrow local optimization snapshot, not the complete canonical collection.
- Tactical-oracle results are bounded fixtures and cannot establish full rules correctness.
- Structural simulations abstract politics, threat perception and many exact card interactions.
