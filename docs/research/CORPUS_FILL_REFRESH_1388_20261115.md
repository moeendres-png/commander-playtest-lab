**Gate reading: the step-5 requirement (not worse than ρ = 0.9715) is met on
every figure** except the ≤ 0.10 count, which moves by one card (240 vs. 241)
because the two window cards below sit outside 0.10 in both runs. The reason
for the improvement is population coverage, not a counting change: the #1387
corpus was a curated, bracket-heavy subset (2,823 of EDHREC's 9,816-deck
population; brackets 4+5 = 41 %). The 7,528 bracket-less decks of the
unclassified cohort are the remainder of EDHREC's own Chulane population, so
the counted population now nearly coincides with the one EDHREC aggregates
over — the corpus numerators track EDHREC's published deck counts almost
exactly (ρ = 0.9999; e.g. the top staple counts 5,989 of 10,128 locally
against EDHREC's 5,839 of 9,816). The level differences of #1387 (casual
staples higher on EDHREC, competitive staples higher in the curated corpus)
collapse with the population gap.

The largest remaining deviations are the window effect of #1387, now isolated
to the cards whose EDHREC denominator is a small recent window while the
corpus divides by the full stock — the corpus numerators match EDHREC's
counts, the shares differ by the denominator alone:

- **Flickering Hound** (EDHREC 0.130 = 164/1,265; corpus 0.017 = 175/10,128;
  Δ −0.112) and **Pia, Aether Ascetic** (0.125 = 160/1,285; corpus 0.016 =
  163/10,128; Δ −0.108): the "New Cards" of the commander page, as in #1387.
- **Badgermole Cub** (0.132 = 768/5,818; corpus 0.079 = 796/10,128; Δ −0.053)
  and **Simulacrum Shaper** (0.044 = 56/1,265; corpus 0.006 = 61/10,128;
  Δ −0.038): the same mechanism over a mid-size window.

## 3. Tier-signal difference, old stand vs. new stand

Method: `strength_view.compute` with default settings plus
`corpus_signals.count_corpus` over the 927 pool cards, run on both fresh
stores of the header. The corpus components are computed from the store decks
at view time, so this measures what the tier list actually consumes.

**Unchanged, as construction requires — measured identical, not assumed.**
The harvest added only bracket-less decks, so every bracket cohort resolves
to the same decks on both stands: denominators tuned (brackets 4+5) 1,066,
low (bracket 2) 608, tournament cohort (bracket 5) 256, and the per-card hit
counts of all three groups are **identical for all 927 pool cards**. At
component level, value, numerator and denominator agree for every card:
`corpus_tuned` (927 cards), `corpus_bracket_slope` (290 cards) and
`tournament` (927 cards) — zero changed values. The lift's EDHREC side did
not move either: `global_inclusion` is identical for all 927 cards, so every
lift delta below is corpus-driven alone.

**`corpus_log_lift` — the one moving signal.** It divides by the whole-corpus
denominator, which grew 2,617 → 10,128:

- Cards with a value: 570 → **741** (+171: a card's lift is undefined without
  a hit — `strength.log_lift_value` — and 171 pool cards now have at least
  one hit in the whole corpus and a positive global share).
- All 570 shared cards change value (the denominator is in every share):
  median Δ −0.016, median |Δ| 0.024, max |Δ| 0.190; 179 move up, 391 down;
  cards above the neutral 0.5 among the shared 570: 165 → 159 (22 cross up,
  28 cross down); over all 741 cards now carrying a value, 188 sit above 0.5. The
  lift compresses toward neutral because the corpus now resembles EDHREC's
  population (section 1) — shares that the curated corpus inflated or
  deflated move toward the global share.
- The largest movers are the old corpus's small-count cards (1–2 hits of
  2,617), whose damped values rested on noise: **Sram, Senior Edificer**
  (1/2,617 → 7/10,128; value 0.409 → 0.218; Δ −0.190), **Duelist's Heritage**
  (1/2,617 → 14/10,128; −0.158), **Hunter's Insight** (1/2,617 → 5/10,128;
  −0.145), **Basilisk Collar** (2/2,617 → 25/10,128; −0.130); largest upward:
  **Steppe Lynx** (2/2,617 → 20/10,128; +0.128).

**Downstream consequence (context, not a signal claim).** The `deck_universe`
component — the local spread itself — also re-bases to the 10,128 decks (740
of 927 shares change), so the tier outcome moves beyond the lift alone: 47 of
927 cards change tier (36 D→C, 6 C→B, 2 B→A, 1 A→S, 1 A→B, 1 B→C; counts
S 38→39, A 33→33, B 44→48, C 154→185, D 653→617, N 5→5); the median index
change is +0.1 points, the largest 5.8. This is the intended effect of
completing the corpus — more complete counted evidence per card — and is
recorded here so the movement is not attributed to the lift alone.
