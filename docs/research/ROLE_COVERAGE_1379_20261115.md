# Role coverage in the Chulane corpus — #1379

Measured 2026-11-15 on branch `muse/data-roles-coverage` (base main `f842b296f`).
Counts only, each with its denominator; no card lists (the per-card detail stays
local, §12). All figures are recomputed from two freshly built stores
(before/after), never estimated.

## Starting point

Issue #1368 measured that `card_role` covers only 5,484 of the 9,041 distinct
cards of the canonical Chulane corpus, and that the role `flicker` exists for
just 7 cards (landfall 154, bounce 30). Root cause, verified against the repo:
the five role files of `data/research/roles_1655/` are scoped to the 1,655
collection cards (tagger bulk crosswalk, tagger search WUG, an external
heuristic, the #1353 tagger expansion, and `roles_s5_oracle_expansion.json` —
the deterministic Oracle-text classifier `roles.classify_oracle_roles` applied
to the collection only). Corpus cards outside the collection could only carry
a role when the WUG tagger search happened to tag them; the Oracle classifier
had never been run over the corpus at all.

## What changed

1. **`roles.classify_oracle_roles` gained the rule classes it lacked** (each
   with positive and negative tests, written red first):
   - `flicker` — "exile …, then return it/that card to the battlefield",
     including the delayed two-sentence form; temporary exile "until ~ leaves
     the battlefield" does not count.
   - `recursion` — "return … from (your/a/an opponent's/any/their/that
     player's) graveyard to the battlefield or your hand", plus the
     "put … from a graveyard onto the battlefield" reanimation form. Returning
     a permanent from the battlefield stays `bounce`.
   - `graveyard_hate` — exiling target/all cards from graveyards or a whole
     graveyard as the effect; exiling cards from your own graveyard as a cost
     (escape and similar) does not count.
   - `tutor` — search revealed into the hand, search put on top of the
     library, and search for a nonland card put onto the battlefield. A land
     onto the battlefield stays `land_ramp`.
   - `tax` — "costs {N} more to cast" on spells in general. A spell's own
     escalating cost formula ("this spell costs {N} more to cast …") is
     excluded: it taxes nobody (review follow-up, see below).
   - `utility_land` — a land with an activated ability whose effect neither
     produces mana nor searches the library (the search case is land ramp).
   - Widened existing rules: `card_draw` also matches "draw cards" and "draw
     that many cards" (the draw step still does not count), with a context
     guard: a draw phrase inside a trigger or replacement condition
     ("whenever you draw a card", "if you would draw a card"), directly
     under a negation ("players can't draw cards") or with an opponent as
     its subject ("target opponent draws a card") is not the controller's
     draw and does not count; `land_ramp` also matches searches naming a
     basic land type ("a Forest card"); the mana branch also matches a tap
     ability whose cost carries an extra component ("{T}, Sacrifice this
     artifact: Add three mana …"). The mana and library-search rules read
     the text with parenthesised reminder passages removed (the convention
     of `layers.py`), so an ability of a created token — a Treasure's mana
     ability, a Lander token's library search — never counts for the card
     that creates the token; `protection` also matches shroud.
   - Probed and deliberately **not** added: a sacrifice-outlet colon pattern
     (0 additional hits in the uncovered residue — every true outlet already
     carries a tagger claim) and a generic "can't" rule (judgment class, see
     residual gap).
2. **`roles_s5_oracle_expansion.json` regenerated** with the extended rules
   (same scope `COLLECTION1655_RULES_1`): 1,570 → 1,734 claims. Against the
   old file 9 claims are removed, none of them a regression: 5 `card_draw`
   (four draw-payoff triggers and one dredge replacement phrase, all
   already matched by the old, narrower pattern) and 4 claims over two
   token creators (2 `ramp`, 1 `mana_dork`, 1 `land_ramp`) whose only
   evidence sat in a token's reminder text. The old file carried the same
   errors; the guards remove them.
3. **New file `roles_s6_oracle_corpus.json`** (scope `CHULANE_CORPUS_RULES_1`,
   origin `ORACLE_TEXT_REVIEW`): the same classifier applied to all 8,973
   distinct oracle ids of the canonical corpus — 10,548 claims over 29 roles.
   Both files are produced by the new committed generator
   `scripts/generate_oracle_role_file.py`, so regeneration is reproducible.
4. **Review follow-up.** An independent review of this change's first version
   found two systematic false-positive classes (the unguarded `card_draw`
   phrases and the reminder-text reading of the mana rule, whose first
   quality note wrongly claimed 0 affected claims) and four disclosure
   gaps. The guards in item 1, the corrected s5 statement in item 2 and the
   composition notes after the coverage table are the fixes; every figure
   below is the fixed state, recomputed from the regenerated files and a
   freshly built store.

## Coverage before/after

Universe: the canonical corpus (2,823 decks via the repo's own
`universe.resolve_universe`, commander-bound, duplicates once), card set =
main/mainboard rows with quantity > 0. 9,041 distinct keys, of which 8,973
carry an oracle id and 68 are name-only rows without one (no role is possible
for those). Denominator below: 8,973 oracle ids.

**Cards with at least one role: 5,484 / 8,973 (61.1 %) → 7,589 / 8,973
(84.6 %).** Uncovered: 3,489 → 1,384. `card_role` rows 17,739 → 23,820;
claims 23,042 → 33,754 (role files 21,272 → 31,984, tagger bulk 1,770
unchanged). Pool coverage (927 pool cards) is unchanged at 817 / 927 — the
gap was never in the pool; the new rules add pool peers only in ramp
(110 → 117), land_ramp (61 → 68), tax (16 → 17) and protection (60 → 61).

Quality controls on the generated claims (local, counts only published):
a scan of all 266 `utility_land` claims of s6 finds 0 cards whose every
ability is mana or search only; samples of the flicker, recursion,
graveyard_hate, tutor and tax claims were read against their Oracle texts.
For the review follow-up, every claim the guards removed was read against
its Oracle text: 44 `card_draw` claims (36 draw phrases inside trigger or
replacement conditions, 2 negations, 6 with an opponent as the drawing
subject), 36 `ramp`/`land_ramp` claims whose only evidence sat in a token's
reminder text — of the first version's 598 s6 `ramp` claims, 12 had their
only "add" inside reminder text (11 removed; one Saga keeps `ramp` through
its own real library search) and 11 more rested on a Lander token's reminder
search — plus 11 `mana_dork`/`mana_rock` follow-on claims on the same cards,
and 3 `tax` claims on spells' own cost formulas. Because the mana and search
rules now match against the reminder-stripped text, no remaining claim can
rest on reminder text; that is a property of the rule, not a scan result.

Composition notes (review follow-up disclosures, counted from the files by
re-applying the rules to each claimed card's text):

- Of the 159 `tutor` claims of s6, **71** rest only on a search that names
  land words (a land card or a basic land type) and puts the found card into
  the hand. The role's growth is therefore mostly land tutoring. The
  asymmetry is in the rules themselves: the same search onto the battlefield
  is `land_ramp`, into the hand it is `tutor`.
- Of the 266 `utility_land` claims of s6, **20** are lands whose only
  non-mana activated ability is cycling or a channel ability (counted by
  removing the cycling and channel lines and re-testing the utility rule).
  They meet the rule as written; "utility land" in the deck-building sense
  is usually more.

## Production impact

`card_role` is read inside the Studio only: `builder.py`, `catalog.py`,
`data_report.py`, and `strength.py` / `strength_view.py` (the function rank
groups), and `corpus_deck_role` — rebuilt from the claims at every role
import — feeds corpus analyses such as #1368's. So yes, this changes
production-visible behaviour of the Studio: more cards show roles in the
builder, and the "same function" ranking view now has role peers for 84.6 %
of the corpus instead of 61.1 % (the default pool ranking does not read
roles). The simulator does not read the Studio `card_role` table; its
`CardRole` in `agents/card_knowledge.py` is a separate concept derived from
card facts. No claim's origin changed: everything added is
`ORACLE_TEXT_REVIEW`, which never overwrites a tagger claim in `card_role`
(origin rank is unchanged).

## Residual gap — what a text rule cannot derive, and why

1,384 of 8,973 corpus cards (15.4 %) still carry no role, plus the 68
name-only keys (no oracle id, hence no facts — a corpus-import residue, not a
role problem). Composition of the 1,384 (measured, each card under its
first matching type in the order creature, enchantment, land, artifact,
sorcery, instant): 551 creatures and 91 legendary creatures, 202
enchantments, 174 lands, 135 sorceries, 135 artifacts, 88 instants, the
rest mixed types. The reasons are principled, not extraction failures:

- **Keyword-only and vanilla cards** (229 with short, keyword-only text of
  at most 60 characters): the taxonomy has no role for trample, haste,
  vigilance, deathtouch or lifelink, and a vanilla body does nothing a role
  names.
- **Mana-only lands** (174 of the residual are lands, 134 of them with no
  line beyond mana production or entering tapped once reminder text is
  removed): a land whose only ability produces mana (or that merely enters
  tapped) has no role by taxonomy design; `utility_land` is the only land
  role.
- **Judgment classes** (the bulk of the 1,155 with longer text): `hate`
  ("players can't …" statics), `card_advantage` and `cantrip` are net-card
  judgments — they depend on mana value and context, which the classifier's
  signature (type line + Oracle text) deliberately does not weigh. Five
  roles still have no Oracle rule at all: `card_advantage`, `cantrip`,
  `hate`, `mana_sink`, `sacrifice_outlet`. Deriving them from text would
  mean guessing, which the classifier's contract ("only clear Oracle text
  mechanics") forbids; they remain tagger-fed.
- One-off triggered and static abilities that match no role's mechanic.

Closing this residue would need either new taxonomy roles (an owner
decision, as in #1261) or curated claims — never a broader regex.

## Reproduction

```bash
python -m commander_lab.studio --data "<data>" import-all \
    --commander "Chulane, Teller of Tales" --from-repo \
    --scryfall "<oracle-cards.jsonl.gz>" --expect-pool 927
python scripts/generate_oracle_role_file.py --data "<data>" \
    --universe collection --out data/research/roles_1655/roles_s5_oracle_expansion.json
python scripts/generate_oracle_role_file.py --data "<data>" \
    --universe corpus --out data/research/roles_1655/roles_s6_oracle_corpus.json
```

Coverage is counted over the store's `card_role` against the canonical
universe above (`universe.resolve_universe`, main/mainboard, quantity > 0).
