## High power / cEDH structure benchmark

Fresh research was checked only for transferable structural functions. Current Rograkh/Ishai competitive material continues to provide a useful structure benchmark for cheap interaction, Rograkh-enabled mana efficiency and compressed/protected finish windows.

- broad answers are reserved for developed go-wide / multiple-engine states;
