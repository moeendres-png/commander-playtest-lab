# Opponent Ensemble Limitations

- Real imported local games: **0**.
- Ensemble weights are therefore unweighted; no arbitrary percentages are presented.
- Cosmic Spider-Man, Morcant Elves, and upgraded Doom lists remain incomplete.
- Variant roles, speed, interaction, wipes, and win axes are explicit synthetic assumptions unless supported by a cited source.
- Empty known-card and assumed-card arrays mean that no card-level assertion is made.
- The official Doom precon variant is a separate reference baseline and is not overwritten by upgrade variants.
- Matchup scores are structural model estimates, not empirical winrates.
- No variant is automatically promoted to an observed profile.
- No deck change is automatically applied.
