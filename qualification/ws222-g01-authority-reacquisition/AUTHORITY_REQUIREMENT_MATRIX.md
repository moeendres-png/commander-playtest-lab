## Scope decisions (from contract, not convenience)

- FULL bulk Oracle (every Magic card, ~30k+ Gatherer pages) is NOT required by
  the canonical contract: G01's Oracle pin serves the admitted/qualification
  domain, and the only card-bearing fixtures are the 29. Bulk capture without
  a requirement/value reason is forbidden by the workstream contract.
- Frozen-29-only capture is SUFFICIENT for the G01 minimum (R-OR-1) because no
  other fixture bears a card identity; deck-legality scope (87+77) is covered
  by R-CMD-1/R-DECK-1 checks, not by full Oracle capture of all deck cards.
  Production-reachable expansion beyond the denominator follows the documented
  extension procedure (Model B extended), or Model C transport separation, or
  a Coordinator waiver (Model D, not granted here).
